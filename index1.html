TEMPORAL DISPLACEMENT THEORY

Time is not a straight line. It is a multi-layered field of parallel micro-realities.

1. The Layered Architecture

TDT proposes that time does not flow like a river. Instead, it exists as a static "stack of panes." Each pane represents a near-identical version of the present moment. Our bodies stay put, but our awareness shifts subtly across these layers.

We create the illusion of flow by moving our consciousness through these static structures, like a projector running through film frames.

3. Identity as the Anchor

Identity is the mechanism that selects which layer you inhabit. A strong sense of self creates a consistent timeline. A fragmented self leads to "drifting," where memories lose cohesion and reality feels unstable.

4. Echo Phenomena

Echoes are not supernatural; they are perceptual artifacts of displacement. When awareness slips through a seam, it picks up data from a neighboring layer.

  • 1

    Déjà Vu

    Overlap with a layer where the event occurred seconds earlier.

  • 2

    Premonition / Instinct

    Brushing a layer where a danger has already played out.

  • 3

    False Memories

    Genuine memories recorded from a different pane/layer.
